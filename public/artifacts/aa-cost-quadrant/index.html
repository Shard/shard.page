Tokens per request applies only to plans that count requests regardless of size (Qwen, MiniMax). Anthropic, OpenAI, Z.ai and Kimi meter consumption, so a bigger prompt just burns the budget faster and their plan value is invariant — that is why moving this slider does not make Claude or ChatGPT look cheaper. Dollar-credit plans (Cursor, Copilot) are unaffected by all of these. Lower utilisation raises your real cost per task, because you paid for headroom you didn't use.
